# Week 5

**Dates:** 06-22 to 06-26

## Goals
- Generte datasets for every taper slice.
- Develop a format suitable for future overlap calculations. 

## Approach and Implementation
Automated the generation of finite-element solutions for every taper slice. Stored the modal field solutions together with the corresponding mesh information. Began investigating methods for converting the irregular finite-element fields into a common representation suitable for mode overlap calculations.

## Results
Succesfully generated datasets for every taper slice. Identified that storing the complete finite-element mesh within even mode produced unnecessrily large files, motivating the development of a more effiient storage strategy.
